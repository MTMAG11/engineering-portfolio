## Project Direction

The overall architecture of the project remains centered around an autonomous rocket using onboard state estimation, guidance, and feedback control. The eventual system is intended to use a combination of sensors such as an IMU, barometer, GPS, and other measurements to estimate the rocket's state.

The long-term landing concept uses thrust vector control and actuating aerodynamic fins rather than relying on a parachute. The simulator will eventually become an important environment for developing and testing the guidance and control algorithms before attempting real-world testing.
